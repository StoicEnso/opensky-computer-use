# OpenSky: positioning and launch copy

Created 19 October 2026; revised the same day to lead with the owner’s ambition: the feeling of having a team of agents. Grounded in implemented capabilities. Audience response and conversion have not yet been measured.

## The person we want to reach

An independent founder, developer or operator who already uses AI agents and wants to accomplish more with a small team. They can describe the outcome. They already have accounts, tools and a Mac. Their frustration is becoming the person who transfers every answer, opens every app and shepherds every step.

They want the feeling of running a capable team, with their own machine and their own choices. Lead with ambition, time and ownership. Let the technical proof earn their trust immediately afterward.

## The promise

**Build like you have a team.**

OpenSky gives your agents a way to operate the Mac you already use: its native apps, your existing Chrome session and supported background tabs. You set the task and its limits. Your agents handle the legwork.

The emotional territory is personal agency: a builder who can put ideas into motion without turning their day into a queue of clicks. The product category is **open-source Mac control for AI agents**.

## The demo that earns the promise

Show one continuous, clearly labelled demonstration: the owner types in their own window while an agent fills a disposable inactive Chrome tab and uploads a sample file. Open the tray to show real in-flight clients, then Pause and verify an attempted action is blocked. Resume, finish the fixture and show the result. End with the install command and source link.

Caption the environment, exact version and supported path. Use test clients honestly; distinguish connected clients from every agent that might be thinking elsewhere. Do not present fixture results as universal website success.

## Claims discipline

Support the confidence with observable behavior. Do not claim universal background control, every harness live-tested, zero data stored, automatic GDPR compliance, bypassed OS consent, unlimited provider access, or freedom from a connected agent’s applicable rules. OpenSky’s local counters omit page content; requested captures and local diagnostics can persist, and observations go to the connected agent. Keep the full details in the linked privacy and tested-scope documents.

## First messaging experiment

Compare “Build like you have a team” with “Give your agents hands.” Use the same demo and install destination. Measure qualified installation starts, completed onboarding, first verified workflow and return use—not likes alone. Treat the founder/operator audience as a hypothesis until interviews and usage support it. Do not invent traction or time-saved numbers.
